Email Send to:

Welcome - A user's first Google sign-in (never again afterwards)
Order confirmed - The server verifies a payment
Payment failed - Razorpay confirms a payment attempt failed
Order status update - An admin changes the status (Processing, Shipped, Delivered)
Order cancelled - The customer or an admin cancels, or an item sells out after payment


How sending works:

Each email is sent only once. Every email gets a unique key, such as order-confirmed:<orderId>, recorded in the emailevents collection. If the same event fires again (a page refresh, a webhook retry), the email is skipped.
Email problems never affect orders. If Resend fails or is down, the error is logged and the email is marked failed, but the payment and order stay correct.
It works without a Resend key. If RESEND_API_KEY is empty, emails are logged as [email] Skipped ... Resend is not configured and everything else keeps working.

Checking that emails go out:

Watch the server terminal for [email] Sent "welcome" (or Failed … / Skipped …).
Open the emailevents collection in MongoDB Atlas; each email shows as sent, failed or skipped.
Check the Emails page in the Resend dashboard.
If RESEND_FROM_EMAIL is onboarding@resend.dev, Resend only delivers to the email address of your own Resend account. Sign in to FitStore with that same Google account to receive them, or verify your own domain in Resend to email anyone.
